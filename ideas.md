<idea>
  <design_movement>Electric Noir / Cyber-Wedding</design_movement>
  <core_principles>
    1. **High Contrast & Energy**: Reflecting the dynamic nature of a DJ set, using deep blacks and vibrant neon accents.
    2. **Fluid Motion**: The interface should feel like a continuous mix, with smooth transitions and rhythmic animations.
    3. **Immersive Atmosphere**: Creating a "night out" vibe right from the landing page, but keeping it elegant enough for weddings.
    4. **Bold Confidence**: Showcasing the DJ's expertise through strong visual statements and assertive typography.
  </core_principles>
  <color_philosophy>
    A base of **Midnight Black** and **Deep Charcoal** to represent the venue at night. Accents of **Electric Purple**, **Neon Blue**, and **Hot Pink** to symbolize the lights, energy, and party atmosphere. This isn't just "dark mode"; it's a "party mode" that feels premium and exciting.
  </color_philosophy>
  <layout_paradigm>
    **Asymmetric Split-Screen**: Breaking away from the standard top-down scroll. Sections might slide in from the sides or reveal themselves through masks. A focus on large, full-bleed imagery with text overlaying in unexpected but readable positions.
  </layout_paradigm>
  <signature_elements>
    1. **Soundwave Visualizers**: Subtle, animated audio waveforms that react to scroll or hover, reinforcing the audio aspect.
    2. **Glow Effects**: Buttons and key headers have a soft, neon glow, mimicking stage lighting.
    3. **Glassmorphism**: Semi-transparent cards for text content, floating over the dynamic background.
  </signature_elements>
  <interaction_philosophy>
    Interactions should feel "tactile" and "responsive," like pressing a button on a mixer. Hover states are snappy and bright. Scrolling feels like moving through a playlist.
  </interaction_philosophy>
  <animation>
    - **Entrance**: Elements fade in with a slight upward drift and a "glitch" or "flash" effect for key headings.
    - **Scroll**: Parallax effects on background layers to create depth.
    - **Hover**: Buttons pulse or shift color rapidly.
  </animation>
  <typography_system>
    - **Headings**: *Syncopate* or *Orbitron* (uppercase, wide tracking) for a futuristic, tech-forward look.
    - **Body**: *Montserrat* or *Lato* (clean, geometric sans-serif) for high readability against dark backgrounds.
  </typography_system>
</idea>

  <core_principles>
    1. **Clean & Airy**: Emphasizing the "Wedding" aspect with a sophisticated, high-end bridal magazine aesthetic.
    2. **Nature-Inspired**: Drawing from the Utah landscape (mountains, salt flats) to ground the local connection.
    3. **Professional Polish**: A focus on trust and reliability, crucial for wedding vendors.
    4. **Content-First**: Letting the images of happy couples and packed dance floors tell the story.
  </core_principles>
  <color_philosophy>
    A palette inspired by Utah weddings: **Sage Green**, **Dusty Rose**, **Slate Blue**, and **Warm Sand**, anchored by **Crisp White** and **Soft Gray**. This evokes a sense of calm, romance, and natural beauty, positioning the DJ as a seamless part of a perfect day.
  </color_philosophy>
  <layout_paradigm>
    **Editorial Grid**: Structured, magazine-style layouts with generous whitespace. Images and text blocks overlap slightly to create depth but maintain a rigid underlying grid for order and elegance.
  </layout_paradigm>
  <signature_elements>
    1. **Thin Lines & Borders**: Delicate 1px borders and dividers to separate sections with elegance.
    2. **Serif Accents**: Using a serif font for numbers or small accents to add a touch of tradition.
    3. **Soft Shadows**: Deep, diffuse shadows on images to make them lift off the page.
  </signature_elements>
  <interaction_philosophy>
    Subtle and smooth. Buttons fill gently on hover. Links have a simple underline animation. The goal is to feel "effortless" and "stress-free," mirroring the service promise.
  </interaction_philosophy>
  <animation>
    - **Entrance**: Slow, graceful fades. Images might scale down slightly as they enter the viewport.
    - **Scroll**: Smooth scrolling with sticky elements (like the booking button) that glide into place.
  </animation>
  <typography_system>
    - **Headings**: *Playfair Display* (elegant serif) for a touch of class and romance.
    - **Body**: *Inter* or *Source Sans Pro* (neutral sans-serif) for clarity and modern simplicity.

<text>
<idea>
  <design_movement>Bold Industrial / Warehouse Party</design_movement>
  <core_principles>
    1. **Raw & Authentic**: Stripping away the fluff to focus on the equipment, the sound, and the raw energy of a dance floor.
    2. **Structured Chaos**: Using heavy grids and blocky layouts to organize information, but breaking them with large typography.
    3. **Impactful**: High-contrast, large-scale elements that demand attention.
    4. **Gear-Focused**: subtly highlighting the technical capability (speakers, lights) as a selling point of quality.
  </core_principles>
  <color_philosophy>
    **Concrete Gray**, **Safety Orange** (or a vibrant **Electric Blue**), and **Matte Black**. This palette feels utilitarian but exciting, like a backstage pass or a high-end equipment case. It suggests reliability and power.
  </color_philosophy>
  <layout_paradigm>
    **Bento Box / Modular Grid**: Content is organized into distinct rectangular blocks or "cards" with thick borders. This works well for listing services, equipment, and pricing features.
  </layout_paradigm>
  <signature_elements>
    1. **Thick Borders**: Heavy strokes around buttons and sections.
    2. **Monospace Accents**: Using a monospace font for technical details or metadata (dates, locations).
    3. **Halftone Patterns**: Subtle dot patterns in the background to add texture without noise.
  </signature_elements>
  <interaction_philosophy>
    Direct and mechanical. Buttons have a "press" effect (transform: scale). Hover states invert colors instantly (no fade).
  </interaction_philosophy>
  <animation>
    - **Entrance**: Elements slide in sharply from the bottom or side.
    - **Scroll**: Sections might "stick" and stack on top of each other (sticky stacking cards).
  </animation>
  <typography_system>
    - **Headings**: *Oswald* or *Teko* (condensed sans-serif) for a tall, impactful headline style.
    - **Body**: *Roboto Mono* (for details) mixed with *Roboto* (for long text).
  </typography_system>
</idea>
</text>
